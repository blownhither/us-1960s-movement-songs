# Soundtrack of the Sixties (`us-1960s-movement-songs`)

> **An interactive historical archive and curated index of all songs, protest anthems, spirituals, and musical works referenced in Terry H. Anderson's acclaimed history *The Movement and the Sixties* (《美国1960年代》).**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub%20Pages-Live%20Demo-brightgreen?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://blownhither.github.io/us-1960s-movement-songs/)

## 📖 Overview

In the turbulent decade of the 1960s in the United States, music was far more than background entertainment for suburban living rooms. As Terry H. Anderson demonstrates in *The Movement and the Sixties*, music functioned as:
- **A weapon for civil rights mobilization** (from southern church freedom songs to Lincoln Memorial mass rallies)
- **The moral voice of the anti-Vietnam War movement** (from Greenwich Village topical folk to active-duty GI underground revues)
- **The spiritual adhesive of the counterculture** (from San Francisco psychedelic ballrooms to the mud of Woodstock)
- **A catalyst for Second-Wave Feminism, Black Power, and Chicano self-determination**

This project provides an **interactive, searchable single-page web application (`index.html`)** that catalogs **143 textual citations and 119 distinct musical works** across the book, pairing every song with:
1. **Book page citations & exact text quotations**
2. **Surrounding historical narrative and events**
3. **In-depth research on 1960s US politics, civil rights, and constitutional struggles**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Instant Full-Text Search**: Real-time searching across Chinese & English titles, artists, lyrics, book quotes, and historical topics (e.g. `Bob Dylan`, `Beatles`, `Vietnam`, `Selma`, `Feminism`, `Woodstock`).
- **One-Click Movement Filters**:
  - ✊ **Civil Rights Movement (民权运动)** (*We Shall Overcome*, *This Little Light of Mine*, *Ain't Gonna Let Nobody Turn Me 'Round*)
  - ☮️ **Anti-War Movement (反战运动)** (*Blowin' in the Wind*, *Masters of War*, *I-Feel-Like-I'm-Fixin'-to-Die Rag*, *Give Peace a Chance*)
  - 🌸 **Counterculture & Psychedelia (反主流文化)** (*San Francisco*, *Sgt. Pepper*, *White Rabbit*, *Eight Miles High*, *Hair*)
  - ♀️ **Women's Liberation (妇女解放)** (*I Am Woman*, *Tapestry*, *Stupid Girl / Under My Thumb*)
  - 📻 **Cold War & 1950s Conformity (冷战与50年代)** (*Sh-Boom*, *Little Boxes*, *Rock Around the Clock*)
  - ⛪ **Gospel, Hymns & Jesus Movement (宗教与灵歌)** (*The Lord's Prayer*, *Godspell*, *Jesus Christ Superstar*)
- **Expandable In-Depth Records**: Click any row to reveal the exact book quotation, chapter context, and historical/political analysis.
- **Responsive & Modern UI**: Built with modern Tailwind CSS, supporting dark and light display modes.
